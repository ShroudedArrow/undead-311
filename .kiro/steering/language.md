# Language, tone, and design rules

Voice and banned phrases: see `voice.md`. Visual rules: see `design-system.md`.

- Say "possible zombie", never "the city lied" or "fake fix". Say measured counts are a floor.
- CityScore® and BOS:311 are city marks: use the names only to describe them; never imply affiliation or name our metric after them. No city seal.
- Aggregate to departments, neighborhoods, districts. Never name or identify individual workers.
- Every claim links to its evidence (case records, numbers, method).
- Tone: spooky but factual. Spooky visuals must never hide information; every animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion` and can be skipped.
- Autopsy card fields: Cause of death (closure label), Time in the grave (gap days), Resurrection date, Confidence, Skeptic's objections, both case records.
- Show uncertainty (intervals) next to every rate.
- Provide Spanish for evidence cards and reports (should-have).
- No third-party logos or trademarks in the UI or video (blur official city marks and chat-app branding in screenshots).
